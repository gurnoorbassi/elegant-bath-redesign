More than 20 years with fixtures

Elegant Bath has over 20 years specifying and sourcing kitchen and bath products.

The showroom stocks hard-to-find plumbing fixtures for custom homes and multi-unit projects. Staff help builders, designers, and homeowners pick parts.

If it is not good enough for our own homes, we would not put it in yours.
